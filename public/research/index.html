A second brain for the principal architect.

Imagine a canvas — like a digital studio wall. Every project is a card on it. Open a card and inside lives everything for that project:

  • Moodboards & references — images, swatches, materials, pinned like a studio wall
  • Material decision log — every approval with photo, date, and who said yes. No more WhatsApp scroll-hunting
  • Drawing shelf — version-controlled, latest-approved always pinned on top
  • The stuck-tracker — what's pending at the structural office, the municipal authority, the client
  • Morning brief — one sentence each morning: what needs your decision today, what's stuck, what your 4 PM client expects

Your whole team on it — everyone sees their tasks when they open it in the morning, you see the bird's-eye of everything, live.
