Material study 07 · Rotary switch

A switch you turn

A two-position rotary switch: a bar knob sitting in a round, recessed well. No numbers. A short line at the end of the bar is red while it points left, off, and turns green when you turn the knob half a turn to the right, on. Choose a material below.

05

Plastic and aluminium, side by side

Same switch, same half turn. Turn both and notice what each material does with the same movement.

MomentPlasticAluminium
TurnSmooth, light, follows the finger closelyHeavier, resists until halfway
TimingEases in and out, 260 msAccelerates into the stop, 160 ms
ArrivalSettles gently, no bounceStops dead
IndicatorPainted line fades from red to greenInlaid line snaps from red to glowing green
SoundA dull, rounded clickA sharp click with a short ring
Shared rulesTap turns it · drag past halfway commits · red line on the left is off, green line on the right is on · Space, Enter and the arrow keys · announced as a switch
