The conclusions

What the data actually told me.

→

Build the dependency tracker first.

The architects converged on this independently. Anything else is roadmap.

10/15
↘

Moodboards are the vitamin, not the painkiller.

Architects already have Pinterest & WhatsApp. They're not moving.

₹

Pricing is a behaviour question, not a survey question.

Surveys find candidates. Only a paid pilot tells you the truth.

+

The roadmap came from buyers' mouths.

Money tracking, task accountability, client approvals — all unprompted asks.
